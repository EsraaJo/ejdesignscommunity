About EJ Designs Community

A space for architects, designers, and creative minds to connect, share ideas, and showcase their work.

THE CREATOR

Created by M.Arch. Esra'a E. Al-Johari

I’m Esra’a E. Al-Johari, a BIM Architect with over eight years of experience in the construction industry, specializing in BIM project delivery. I hold an M.Sc. in Architecture and Urban Planning and a B.Sc. in Architectural Engineering from the University of Jordan.

The idea for EJ Designs Community began in 2021: a platform where architects and designers can connect, exchange ideas, and showcase their work. I created this community to make creative collaboration and professional connection more accessible. Members can share freelance opportunities, exchange professional services, and find collaborators for architecture projects.
